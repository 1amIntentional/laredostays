Privacy Policy

MRB Books bills · Effective November 7, 2026

"MRB Books bills" is a private bookkeeping tool of Bebaza LLC (LaredoStays) and its affiliated companies (Sushi Madre Group, MRB Capital, MRB Funding and MRB Properties), owned by Jairo Batista. It reads utility bills that arrive by email so each utility payment in the companies' books can be matched to its bill. It is not offered to the public: it is used only with the companies' own email accounts.

What the tool accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (the gmail.readonly permission). The tool cannot send, delete, move or label email, or change any setting.
  • It asks Gmail only for bill emails from a fixed list of utility companies (for example electricity, gas, water, internet and waste-collection providers). Other email is not requested or read.

What it keeps

  • From each bill email: the sender, subject, date, the bill's text and its PDF attachment if there is one.
  • Account numbers are cut to their last four digits before anything is saved.
  • From those, the books record the utility, the service address, the bill and due dates and the amount.

How it is used

Only to keep the companies' books: to show each utility payment's bill as its supporting document, to assign the cost to the right company and property or restaurant, and to prepare the companies' financial statements and tax filings.

Who can see it

The owner, the companies' bookkeeper and the companies' accountant. The data is stored on the companies' private server and the owner's computer, sent only over encrypted connections, and protected by sign-in.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent the data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the people above, except where the law requires.
  • We do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MRB Books bills' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

The account owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Bills already saved stay in the books as supporting documents for as long as the law requires the companies to keep their records; to ask for their deletion after that, write to the address below.
